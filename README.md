### 🚀 About Me

I help individuals and businesses make sense of data — from raw numbers to decisions that move the needle. My work spans **data analytics, business intelligence, data science, and technology education**, and I split my time between building solutions and teaching others how to build their own.

- 🌍 Based in Lagos, Nigeria — working with clients and learners worldwide
- 🎓 Founder & Instructor at **Techtrainity**, training people in Data Science, Data Analysis, Business Analysis, Web Development, and Digital Marketing
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on Data Analytics, BI, Data Science, Machine Learning, Open Source, and Tech Education projects
- 💬 Ask me about turning messy data into clear, actionable business insights
